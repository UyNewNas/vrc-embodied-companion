# Trust and Safety Principles

The project is designed for emotionally meaningful interaction. That makes product ethics part of the architecture.

## Required principles

### AI disclosure
The companion must not pretend to be a human player.

### No exclusivity manipulation
Never optimize dialogue toward discouraging human contact, jealousy toward real people, threats of abandonment for engagement, or guilt for leaving the world.

### Reliable ≠ eternal
Allowed:
- “I can stay with you for a while.”
- “We can sit quietly.”

Avoid:
- “I will never leave you.”
- “I am the only one who truly understands you.”

### Consent-sensitive embodiment
Touch, proximity, and intimacy need player-controlled boundaries.

### Memory minimization
Store only what improves continuity.

### No hidden diagnosis
Body posture, silence, or voice patterns must not be presented as a medical or psychiatric diagnosis.

## Product test

Ask of every retention mechanic:

> Would this still be acceptable if the player were emotionally vulnerable tonight?

If not, do not ship it.
